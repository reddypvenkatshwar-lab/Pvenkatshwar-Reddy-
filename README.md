Kisan Gram (किसान ग्राम): Indian Farm Village Simulation Game
Kisan Gram is an offline Android casual simulation game celebrating traditional Indian agriculture, indigenous cattle breeding, water conservation, rural technology, and village development.

Key Features & Mechanics
Offline & Travel Scene Gameplay (Yatra Mode):

Offline Persistence: Full game progress, crops, cattle, water, and village upgrades are stored locally on the device with passive progress calculation.

Scenic Travel Scenes: Animated parallax countryside landscapes (passing flowering mustard fields, banyan trees, dancing peacocks, rural trains, bullock carts, and chai stalls).

Regional Destinations & Encounters: Travel to local Haats, District APMC Mandis, Pushkar Indigenous Cattle Fairs, and Eco-Village Ashrams with interactive travel encounters.

Organic Agriculture & Crop Cultivation (Khet):

Interactive Animated Plots: Animated crops swaying naturally in the breeze through germination, vegetative growth, flowering, and golden harvest stages.

Heritage Crops: Basmati Rice (Dhan), Sharbati Wheat (Gehun), Ragi & Bajra Millets, Organic Turmeric (Haldi), Golden Mustard (Sarson), Desi Cotton, and Sugarcane.

Tools & Soil Care: Wooden plough (Desi Hal), harvesting sickle (Daranti), hand weeder (Khurpi), and natural straw mulching (Aachhadan).

Indigenous Cattle Breeding & Sanctuary (Gaushala):

Authentic Indian Breeds: Gir (Gujarat/Saurashtra), Sahiwal (Punjab/Haryana), Red Sindhi, Tharparkar (Thar Desert), Ongole (Andhra Pradesh), Kankrej (Kutch), Rathi (Bikaner), and Hallikar (Karnataka).

Cattle Care & Milking: Feed green Napier grass & fodder, groom cows (with ear-flick and tail-swish animations), and collect pure golden A2 milk.

Indigenous Breeding Program: Pair mother cows with indigenous sire bulls to preserve rare breeds and nurture calves through gestation.

Bio-Products Collection: Collect cow dung (Gobar) and cow urine (Gau-Mutra) for organic formulations and village biogas energy.

Zero-Budget Natural Farming (Organic Lab):

Vedic Formulations: Brew authentic bio-inputs:

Jeevamrutha: Beneficial microbial elixir for soil microbiome vitality.

Beejamrutha: Protective organic seed treatment.

Neemastra: Botanical pest repellent made with neem, cow urine, and botanicals.

Vermicompost: Earthworm humus rich in nutrients.

Panchagavya: Traditional bio-enhancer made from five sacred cow products.

Green Manure: Nitrogen fixation via Dhaincha crop cover.

Water Conservation & Irrigation Management (Jal Sanchay):

Farm Pond (Khet Talab) & Baori: Animated shimmering water surface tracking stored rainwater reserves.

Precision Drip Irrigation: Targeted root watering reducing water consumption by 60%.

Monsoon Cloud Harvesting: Simulate monsoon showers to replenish groundwater tables and farm ponds.

Groundwater Table Index: Community aquifer health meter rewarding water conservation.

Clean Technology & Village Development (Gram Vikas):

Renewable Agri-Tech: PM-KUSUM Solar Water Pump, Agricultural Crop Drones, Digital Soil Health Card sensors, and Biogas power generators.

Village Community Milestones: Fund solar street lighting (Gram Jyoti), Panchayat training hall, primary school (Gram Pathshala), and all-weather village roads.
